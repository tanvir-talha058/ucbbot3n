About UCB Bank

United Commercial Bank Limited (UCB) is one of the leading private commercial banks in Bangladesh, incorporated in 1983. UCB provides a wide range of banking and financial services through its 200+ branches and 500+ ATMs nationwide.

The bank is committed to delivering digital-first banking experiences through UCB iBank (online banking) and UCB mCash (mobile banking).

Interest Rates

Fixed Deposit (1 Year): 6.5% – 7.5% p.a.

Personal Loan: 10% – 14% p.a.

Home Loan: 9% – 11% p.a.

Rates are indicative and subject to change. Please visit a branch for current rates.
